What SLA Credit Lab processes

SLA Credit Lab is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira Service Management. It prepares an SLA-credit calculation and its internal review. It does not issue an accounting credit, payment, or Jira change.

When a Jira admin saves a contract rule, the app stores that rule with the admin's Atlassian account ID and the date. When a user approves a credit on a Jira Service Management request, the app stores the issue key, the amount, the approver's Atlassian account ID and the date. To compute a credit, the app reads the SLA cycles of the current request through Jira's API, with the permissions of the person viewing it; SLA data is not copied into storage.

Free-text fields may contain personal data. Use references instead of names where possible. Never enter passwords, bank details, or sensitive personal data.

Where data is stored

Data is stored only in Atlassian Forge hosted storage, hosted by Atlassian. The app sends no data outside Atlassian, uses no external server, analytics or advertising tool, and writes no logs. The reviewed candidate declares no external fetch (egress), remote service, or web trigger. The app requests three Forge scopes: read:request.sla:jira-service-management, storage:app, and report:personal-data.

Access and recipients

Access to stored rules and approvals is limited to the installation. The app uses Atlassian Forge and Jira, including the Personal Data Reporting API to report retained Atlassian account IDs weekly.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in your site until the app is uninstalled. Account IDs are reported to Atlassian weekly and replaced by "erased" when Atlassian reports an account as closed. Users remain responsible for any exports they copy or download.
